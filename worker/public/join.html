Join a shared list

Someone shares a to-do list with you in Saqme To Do. It stays in step with theirs: what either of you changes shows up for the other.

Use it now, in this browser

Nothing to install, no account, no sync of your own. The list opens here and works offline too. On a phone, add the page to your Home Screen to keep it, and allow notifications to be reminded of its deadlines.

Use it in this browser

You have Saqme To Do

On a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux): Open in Saqme To Do; it asks before it joins. On a phone: Copy invite, open Saqme, then Settings › Sync & sharing, and paste it under Shared lists. Neither needs sync of your own.

Open in Saqme To Do

Install Saqme To Do

Free and open source: your lists are plain markdown files on your computer, no account needed. Install it with one command, then open this link again and choose Open in Saqme To Do.

Windows, in PowerShell:
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francuza9/saqme-to-do/main/scripts/setup.ps1 | iex

macOS, Linux, in a terminal:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francuza9/saqme-to-do/main/scripts/setup.sh | bash
