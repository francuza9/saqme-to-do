# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part here (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and do not harass anyone.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately, at the contact address on their GitHub profile.
Reports are read promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break these rules, and block people who keep doing so.
